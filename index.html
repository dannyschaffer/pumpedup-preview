Water backup that switches itself on

When the supply cuts out, your house won't notice.

A switch-over system sits between your municipal line, your tanks and your borehole. When the municipal pressure drops, it changes over to stored or borehole water automatically, so showers, toilets and the kitchen keep working.

FAQ

Questions we get a lot.

Something else? Send us a WhatsApp and we'll answer.

Do I need a borehole to get water backup?

No. A JoJo tank system with a switch-over and booster pump fills from the municipal line and takes over when the supply cuts out. Adding a borehole later just gives the tanks a second source.

How long does an installation take?

Most full borehole systems are installed in one day. Bigger multi-tank or rewiring jobs can take longer. We'll tell you upfront.

Is borehole water safe to drink?

With the right filtration and UV treatment, yes. For kitchen drinking water we also install reverse osmosis units with their own tap.

Do you drill boreholes?

We do the survey, yield test, pump and full system. For drilling we recommend a trusted drilling company and coordinate with them.

What does a geological survey cost?

R2,000 including VAT, with a full report of our findings.

Can you fix a system someone else installed?

Yes. We repair and service pumps, controllers, switch-overs and filtration, whoever put them in.
